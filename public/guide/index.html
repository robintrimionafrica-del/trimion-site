Common questions

Do I get training?

Yes. Free online operational training comes with every machine. In-person training is available at extra cost, with a male or female trainer.

Can I get certified?

Yes, certification is available separately for R1,500.

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

R150 anywhere in South Africa, 9–12 business days.

How do I pay?

EFT or a secure card link on your invoice. Or use lay-by: a deposit, then 2 or 3 monthly payments.

Will it work with my plug point?

Final voltage and plug type are confirmed on your invoice before dispatch. Some larger machines need a dedicated plug point; we'll tell you if yours does.

What if I need help after I buy?

You get ongoing WhatsApp support on 061 118 5978.

Is it a medical device?

No. These machines are for salon and spa use.

Can I see all your machines?

Yes, the full catalog of 159 machines is at trimionafrica.co.za.
